GETTING STARTED

Guide.

A short overview of how Voz works. Detailed installation instructions will be added before release.

01

Install Voz

Download the Windows installer when the public release is available, then follow the setup assistant.

02

Configure your assistant

Open the Voz control centre and configure your Discord bot, Ollama model and voice settings.

03

Join a voice channel

Start Voz, join a Discord voice channel and speak to your assistant.

Voz uses local AI components. The installer and model downloads may require a substantial amount of disk space and an internet connection during setup.
